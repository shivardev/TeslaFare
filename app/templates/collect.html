Price collector

Open a station on Tesla's site and the TeslaFare userscript saves the price it shows you. Stations on recently planned routes come first. A price stays fresh for 24 hours, then returns to this list.

Only one tab opened: your browser blocked the rest. Choose Allow pop-ups for this site in the bar under the address bar (one time), then try again.

One-time setup
  1. Install Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey in the browser you'll collect with.
  2. Enter your collector key (the COLLECTOR_KEY on this server, or the key you were given). It stays in this browser.
  3. Install the userscript: install TeslaFare collector. The key is built in, so it never asks for it. Reinstall after changing the key.
  4. Click Open next 5. Each Tesla tab saves its price, shows a green note and closes itself. On a single tab, press N for the next station or S to skip one.
